# Porting the preview design into the app: design brief (2026-11)

The game has two UIs. The shipping app (`apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/screens/`) is built on the
original design system; the prototype at `#/preview` ([DESIGN.md](../../apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/preview/DESIGN.md))
is a different look and a different way to play ("front office at night, broadcast on the wall").
The prototype says itself it is "wrong for shipping": mock data, inline English, its own CSS.
Nothing in the roadmap moves it into the app. This brief decides what to keep, what to drop and
how to port it with the game playable after every PR.

I read the code and the prototype's notes. I have not run the app next to the preview, so job 1
starts by taking screenshots of both.

The app's screens use token names (`bg-panel`, `text-muted`, `text-good`), so changing the token
values re-skins every screen at once. That is the cheap first step.

## Decisions

Each has a recommendation; say so if you want another.

1. **No fixed 1920x1080 canvas.** At the app's 1100x700 minimum it would scale to 0.57, so the
   14px body text becomes 8px. Keep the preview's layout rules (a hero plus two or three panels,
   nothing scrolls at a normal size) in a fluid layout that holds from 1280x800 up; below that a
   panel may scroll. _Recommended._
2. **Navigation: fold 15 screens into six sections, keep every screen.** Home; Team (Roster,
   Lines, Cap); Market (Free agency, Trades, Draft); League (Standings, Leaders, Games, News,
   History); Office (Front Office, Owner); Online and the seat switcher live in the bar's corner.
   Preview's Scout becomes a tab of Market on the draft class, Calendar a tab of League. Sections
   keep keys 1-6.
3. **No overall rating.** The spec says "no overall rating" and the sim runs on hidden ratings.
   The preview's OVR mode, its 1-20 attribute groups and "scouted 3/3" fog would add a rating
   scale and a scouting mechanic the sim does not have. Port the role-fit tier label and the
   percentile card the app already has; drop the OVR toggle; fog comes only from what exists
   (potential ranges, the analytics department's blur).
4. **Keep the generated faces.** They are a finished feature; the preview's image files do not
   exist. Scenes and crests fall back to the dark textured frame; art is not part of this work.
5. **Continue never blocks.** A Need only warns, and only when it expires today.
6. **Keep the existing shell features.** The seat switcher, the hand-over curtain, the guest's
   view, the Steam line, help, language and the sim bar's other buttons (to date, to the deadline,
   to opening night, the stop filter) all survive in the new frame. Continue is the default; the
   rest sit in a menu next to it.

## Design

**Tokens first, frame second, screens one at a time.** The palette, type, radius and motion move
into `styles.css`'s `@theme`; the preview's `data-phase` becomes an attribute set from the real
status (`useStatus`: phase, free agency open, draft live, deadline week). The prototype's CSS
(2,232 lines under `.pv`) is not copied: its parts become `ui/` components (Drawer, Peek, Menu,
Needs, phase strip) on the tokens, with strings in `en.ts` and `fr.ts`. French in condensed caps
runs longer; every label is checked in both languages.

**Decision design is the rule set to port,** not the pixels: lead with the conclusion, give every
stat a judgment and a comparison, three levels (glance, peek, full), context picks the facts,
staff speak plainly, highlight only exceptions. The prototype's staff lines come from the
assistant GM and departments that already exist; where the sim has no answer a line is left out,
not invented.

**One screen per PR, the game playable after each.** Until the last screen is ported the old
and new looks sit side by side, so job 1 is the token change alone: every screen takes the new
palette and type together, with no layout change.

**Delete the prototype at the end.** Its mock data, store and CSS go; the design rules move
to `docs/`; the `#/preview` route comes out of `main.tsx`.

## Jobs (one PR each, in order)

1. **Tokens and type.** Screenshots of every screen and of the preview; the new palette, both
   fonts bundled, square radii and phase colours in `@theme`; contrast and colour-blind check of
   the good, warning and bad tones. _Done when:_ every screen renders with no layout change and
   the tones meet WCAG AA on the new surfaces. Sonnet 5.5, medium.
2. **The frame.** Top bar, six sections on keys 1-6, `Cmd+K`, Continue on `Space` with the sim bar's
   other options in a menu, the `data-phase` theme from `useStatus`, and the seat switcher,
   curtain, Online, Steam line, help and language moved in. _Done when:_ all 15 screens are
   reachable, a hot-seat game and a guest's view still work, and the screens run in French.
   Sonnet 5.5, high.
3. **Home.** The hero, "Needs you" (the To do items with their answer on the button, each with a
   keyboard path), the wire, the next three games; the phase variants. _Done when:_ a day's work
   needs no scrolling at 1280x800 and Space sims to the next game. Opus 5.5, high.
4. **The player drawer.** Glance, peek, drawer and right-click menu, on the percentile card and
   role-fit label (no OVR), with the contract's value and the staff's word. _Done when:_ any
   player opens from every list in at most three clicks or keys. Sonnet 5.5, high.
5. **Team.** Roster with the drawer, the Lines board, Cap. _Done when:_ a drag on Lines and a
   send-down on Roster still dispatch the same commands. Sonnet 5.5, high.
6. **Market.** Free agency, Trades (the verdict first, then cap now and next season and the line
   impact), and Scout as the draft class tab. _Done when:_ a trade, an extension and a free-agent
   offer each take the number of clicks the prototype's journeys list (1 to accept, 3 to counter).
   Opus 5.5, high.
7. **Draft room.** The pick run, the 300px clock and the skippable reveal on the existing live
   draft room. _Done when:_ a human team drafts, trades up and is called on the clock as today.
   Sonnet 5.5, high.
8. **League and Office.** Standings (the whole league, not one division), Leaders, Games,
   News, History, Front Office, Owner, Online, on the new frame. _Done when:_ every screen has
   the new layout and opens in under 200 ms on a year-20 save. Sonnet 5.5, medium.
9. **Remove the preview.** Delete `preview/`, the `#/preview` route and its mock data; move the
   design rules into `docs/`; a built-app run (Playwright) through a season, a draft and a
   summer with screenshots checked. _Done when:_ no import of `preview/` remains and `pnpm check`
   passes. Sonnet 5.5, medium.

Out of scope: portrait, scene and crest art, sound, a basketball look, and any new sim mechanic
(OVR, scouting levels).
