# Path to v0.1 (Early Access)

The steps from today's foundation to a first version people can buy. Each milestone ends
with something playable or measurable, and with `pnpm check` green.
Scope and priorities: [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). Feature detail: [FEATURE_SPEC.md](FEATURE_SPEC.md).

**Who does what.** Azmi owns data, models and calibration (`ml/`): the two trained models
(stats to ratings, development), real-world targets for the balance lab, playtest analysis.
Machine learning is used only where a fit is needed; everything else is calibrated rules
([ADR 0005](adr/0005-calibrate-first.md)). Minh owns the sim engines, app, UI and architecture.
Each milestone below says where the split is. The contract between the two tracks is
always an interface in `packages/sim` or a data format in `packages/shared`: agree on it
first, in its own small PR, then both build against it.

**Rule for every milestone:** done means merged, tested, checked in the balance lab where
it touches sim numbers, and visible in the app (even if the screen is plain).

## M1 — Ratings and data contract

Decide what a player _is_, before anything is built on it.

- Azmi: data pipeline v1 (NHL play-by-play, player seasons including junior/Europe,
  draft history, contracts) and analysis of which stats predict goals and wins.
  Current status: a reproducible NHL-only pipeline and season-held-out
  [study](M1_PROTOTYPE_REPORT.md) exist. Junior/European, draft and contract inputs
  are still missing. Do not use a restricted source without the needed rights.
- Both: agree the final hidden rating keys per position from the evidence and game
  design, then update `packages/shared/src/sports/hockey.ts` and the roster schema together.
  Status: list v2 is in code and documented in [RATINGS.md](RATINGS.md) (adds `faceoffs`,
  now calibrated against its measured effect on winning; v1 roster files upgrade).
- Proposed addition: review the [advanced-stat definitions](ADVANCED_STATS.md) that
  will let future game events produce comparable measures. These definitions are
  design work; the current sim does not yet produce full player stat lines.
- Minh: roster JSON import into a new league (fictional default + community file path).
  ✅ Done: **Load roster file…** button, `rosters/fictional-template.json`, `docs/RATINGS.md`.

**Done when:** the rating list is agreed and documented; a roster file loads into a
playable league; Azmi's pipeline produces a clean dataset in `ml/data/processed`
with the planned coverage. The NHL-only prototype is a partial dataset.

**Scope decision (2026-11-15):** junior/European, draft and contract data are deferred past
v0.1. The game ships with community roster files, so real-player data does not ship and is
only needed to test rating choices. M1 is signed off on the NHL-only prototype. Commercial
source rights are still tracked in [M1_DATA_ACCESS.md](M1_DATA_ACCESS.md).

## M2 — Game sim v1 (line by line)

- Minh: lines and pairs, ice time per line, power play / penalty kill units, goalie
  selection rules, injuries during games (basic). Game produces a full box score
  (goals, assists, shots, TOI, +/-, saves) and an event stream with shot locations.
- Azmi: shot-quality targets from NHL data (goal rate by distance, angle and type) to tune the rules `gm-xg-table` behind `GoalModel`.
- Both: tune with the balance lab until every hockey check is in range (targets from the
  research report: 3.0-3.15 goals/team/game, .890-.900 SV%, no playoff momentum modifier).
- Fast "result only" mode for playoff odds.

**Status: engine done** (`sports/hockey/game.ts`). The game is played shift by shift: lines and
pairs by ice time, power-play and penalty-kill units, penalties as they happen, a
pulled goalie and empty-net goals, 3v3 overtime then a shootout (5v5 sudden death in the playoffs),
injuries in games, a backup goalie on back-to-backs and one pulled after four early goals. Goals come
out of shots; nothing is decided first. The box score and shot-location stream are the same format.
`resultOnly` plays the same game without the box score (it is not a separate model).

**GoalModel contract for Azmi:** `GoalModel.shotXg({ x, y, shooting, power })` returns the chance a
shot is a goal against an average goalie (`x` feet from the goal line toward the dots, `y` feet from
the middle of the ice, `power` = shooter's team has a penalty advantage). The engine multiplies in
the goalie. Default: `rulesGoalModel`; compare any trained table against it in the lab (`pnpm lab --xg <file>`) with a fixed seed.
Not modelled: majors and fights, rebounds, blocked shots and misses, user-set lines (M8).

**Done when:** a season's player and team stat lines look like a real NHL season; balance
lab all green over 100 seasons; sim speed still within budget. Games to overtime is now in
range. "Better team wins a best-of-7" is in range on seeds 1/101/201, in new and settled
leagues (the lab judges the better team by its strength as the games play it; new leagues start
with team edges of ±6 rating points).

## M3 — The full season loop

- Season calendar with real structure: 84 games, divisions and conferences, back-to-backs.
- Playoffs: bracket, series, honest momentum (goalie form), playoff cap check.
- Awards (our names) and records.
- Offseason rollover: aging and development (simple rules first), retirements, contract
  expiries, draft (simple), re-signing, free agency (simple), next season's schedule.

**Done when:** one league plays 20 seasons straight with no crash or dead end; the balance
lab runs multi-year checks (league talent stays stable, no runaway dynasties, cap usage sane).

**Status: done.** Schedule with divisions, conferences and back-to-backs; playoffs with NHL-style
wild cards and the playoff cap check; per-game goalie form calibrated on NHL data
(`ml/src/form.py`); awards including a Playoff MVP, and records; the whole offseason. 20 seasons
run in `offseason.test.ts` and the lab's multi-year checks pass. The cup is "Champion" until it
is named. Training camp and a trade deadline that matters wait for trades (M6).

## M4 — Players: identity and development

- Azmi: development model trained on NHL careers (career shapes, skill-specific aging,
  fixed effects for survivor bias), exported as a `RulesDevelopmentConfig`; rates of peaks,
  busts and late bloomers as lab checks. Stats-to-ratings model trained on simulated leagues,
  beating the v1 fit's held-out R² (goalies especially).
- Minh: percentile cards, builds and role tags in the UI, generated faces, injury history.

**Done when:** careers look realistic over 20 simulated years (peaks, declines, busts and
late bloomers at real-world rates); player profile shows percentiles, build, career stats.

**Status (2026-11-16): Minh's side mostly done.** Roster screen with percentile player cards
(ranked within the position group), builds and role tags (hockey rules in
`sports/hockey/builds.ts`, behind `SportModule.role`),
personality traits, generated faces that age, career stats archived each season
(`player_seasons`), Hall of Fame. Not yet: injury history, potential ranges on the profile.
Azmi: development model, stats-to-ratings model, career-shape lab checks.

## M5 — Contracts and cap

- All CBA checks from the ruleset (terms, minimums, max, variance, bonuses, buyouts,
  retention, offseason overage).
  ✅ Done (`sim/cba`): terms, minimum, maximum, variance, entry-level contracts, buyouts (with a
  buyout command and dead cap), retention limits (used by trades in M6), cap ceiling, 10%
  offseason overage, cap floor. Roster files with illegal contracts are rejected; AI teams reach
  the floor. The 23-player maximum holds from opening night (the farm team, minor leagues job 4).
  Not yet: bonuses and 35+ contracts (not modelled).
- Cap sheet with future projection and allocation; cap advisor warnings.
  ✅ Done: data (`capSheet`, hand-checked test), Cap screen with advisor warnings (warn, never block).
- Re-sign and extension flow (negotiation kept simple for v0.1).
  ✅ Re-sign done: `contract.resign` in the offseason window (flat market ask, any legal term, cap what-if on the Cap screen); AI no longer re-signs a human team's players. Not yet: in-season extensions, negotiation.

**Done when:** no illegal contract can be created by players or AI; cap sheet matches a
hand-checked example.

**Status (2026-11-16): done for v0.1 scope.** In-season extensions (an `extension` that starts
when the current deal ends), retained salary in trades (`retained` cap charges), free-agent
signings checked by `validateContract` and the cap year. Not modelled: bonuses, 35+ deals,
RFA offer sheets and arbitration, trade protection.

## M6 — Value and AI GMs

- Azmi: realism checks for the rules value, pick curve and contract asks: published
  trade and deadline anchors (rental tiers, pick prices) turned into lab or spot-check targets.
- Minh: team status (league pulse) feeding the AI; trades v1 (offers, accept/reject with
  reasons, trade block, retention, conditional picks); AI GMs re-sign, sign and trade on
  their own; free agency v1 with priorities and interest meter.

**Done when:** the AI makes trades a hockey fan would call realistic over a 10-season run
(spot-check log); obvious exploits (3-for-1, dumping bad contracts for free) fail.

**Status (2026-11-16): first full pass.** Value v1 (`surplusValuator`: per-team surplus by
status and season left, restricted years, negative contracts) and a pick value curve
(`pickValue`), both rules-based and tuned against published trade anchors. Trades v1
(`sim/trades`): window, ownership, cap, position minimums, depth discount (3-for-1 fails),
bad contracts cost full price (dumps fail), retained salary, draft picks for three drafts,
reasons for every answer. AI-to-AI trades weekly (both GMs must accept; about a quarter use a
pick) and AI offers to you (inbox, a week to answer). Free agency v1 with an interest meter
(money, winning, role, term by age, tilted by greed and ambition). Draft v1 with scouted
ranges for every team and your ranked list. Lab: all checks in range. Not yet: trade block,
conditional picks, AI GM styles. AI teams now offer to free agents day by day and a contested
player picks his best offer (`offseason/market.ts`); the full live bidding war stays post-launch.

## M7 — Living league

- Event engine events: trade requests, slumps and streaks, coach on the hot seat, holdouts.
- League feed, league pulse on team pages, "sim until it matters", playoff odds and race
  nights, power rankings.

**Done when:** simming a season produces a feed you'd actually read, and the sim stops on
the moments that need you.

**Status (2026-11-16): first pass.** Feed with names (trades, signings, extensions, long
injuries, first pick, streaks at 6/9/12, hat tricks and shutouts for your team), playoff odds
and power rating (Pythagorean, Monte Carlo with real seeding), race nights, sim until it
matters (league-wide moments, your team, long injuries to your players, offers to you).
The event engine (trade requests, holdouts, hot seat) and morale came later; see the ROADMAP.
